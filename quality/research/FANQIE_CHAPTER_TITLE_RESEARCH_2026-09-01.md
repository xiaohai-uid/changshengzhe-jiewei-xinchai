# FANQIE CHAPTER TITLE RESEARCH · 2026-09-01

## Scope

Purpose: calibrate chapter-title attractiveness for 《长生者皆为薪柴》 using publicly visible Fanqie title/catalog examples. This research does not own Canon authority and should not cause prose imitation.

## Observed high-attention examples

### 《十日终焉》
Visible directory examples include:
- 空屋
- 说谎
- 灾难？
- 赢面
- 摊牌
- 难以接受的真相
- 结束了？

Pattern: very short; concrete state/action; frequent curiosity gap; occasional question mark; little author explanation.

### 《我不是戏神》
Visible directory examples include:
- 戏鬼回家
- 我们在看着你
- 爸，我饿
- 第二只？
- 你也是个灾厄
- 杀戮舞曲
- 准备好回答我的提问了吗？

Pattern: direct character voice, threat, contradiction, strange concrete statement. Long titles are allowed when the sentence itself creates tension.

### 《天渊》
Visible directory/recent update examples include:

- 悔婚
- 了断因果，莫要后悔

- 收账
- 谈不拢，战！
- 围杀，灭口

Pattern: conflict/result first; verbs and character attitude; title can function as a scene promise.

### 《诡舍》
Visible directory examples include:

- 消失的尸体

- 可怕的三楼
- 瘆人的日记

Pattern: abnormal concrete object/fact produces immediate question.

## Practical conclusions for this project

1. Strong chapter titles do not need to summarize the whole chapter.
2. Curiosity is often created by one abnormal fact, dangerous line, concrete object, or irreversible result.
3. Character voice is commercially stronger than author-theme voice when both are truthful.
4. A question mark is useful when the chapter genuinely asks that question; it should not become a repeated gimmick.
5. Long titles can work if they are spoken/charged sentences; shortness is not the goal by itself.
6. Our recent title pattern overused work instructions and moralized rule sentences.
7. From CH014 onward, generate >=8 final-title candidates across >=3 families and score CLICK/SPECIFIC/VOICE/FRESH/HONEST.
