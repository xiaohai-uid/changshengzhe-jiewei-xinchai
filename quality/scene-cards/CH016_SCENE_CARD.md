## 1. What does Chen want right now?

He wants to keep the new formal herb-worker position and turn its food/resource stability into actual bodily growth. At the same time he cannot let the next seven days produce an obviously impossible recovery curve.

## 2. What blocks him on scene?

Formal-worker status immediately brings heavier real work. The arm is becoming more durable, and Xu is required to record injury/recovery every day. The same improvement Chen wants is now a visible variable.

## 3. What does he know / not know?

Knows:
- the arm's range and muscle state have already been recorded;
- the next seven days will continue to be recorded;
- extra food reached him and reduces the acute hunger cost of labor/cultivation;
- prior true qi loss correlated with persistent improvement.

Does not know:
- what recovery rate pharmacy would judge abnormal;
- the complete body-cultivation mechanism;
- whether one skipped cultivation night meaningfully changes the long-term curve;
- why the worm-aura test remains weak but nonzero.

## 4. What choice must he make under incomplete information?

He separates **total work output** from **visible right-arm load**: carry smaller loads but make more trips, paying with time/legs/back. After Xu records increased endurance, Chen deliberately refuses an available qi-interception/cultivation opportunity for one night instead of maximizing growth.

This is a risk-management choice, not a solved formula. He does not know the perfect safe speed.

## 5. What does he actually gain?

- evidence that the body's improvement is not only arm elevation: the same arm position can repeat work longer and recover use sooner;
- the formal ration materially supports sustained labor;
- he keeps the public arm-height curve nearly flat for the day;
- Xu expands his work access from general outer-shed labor to helping sort new seedlings in the east shed.

## 6. Bidirectional cost

- lower load means more trips; legs/back take more fatigue;
- he gives up a real night when cultivation conditions and food support are favorable;
- continuous reporting still remains and may become more dangerous precisely because endurance is improving.

## 7. Net scene change

Start: Chen has a new job/ration but does not yet know how to live with the recovery record.
End: he has a first practical way to **budget visible growth**, retains a genuine endurance gain, and gains a new work-access step. He is not safer in absolute terms; he has more agency.

## 8. What new problem grows from the gain?

The east-shed sorting task gives him access to incoming seedlings and a more valuable production layer. More access means more opportunity to learn why herbs are valued, but also more responsibility for mistakes and losses.

Other workers continue their own pace, shortcuts and complaints; none is given a one-off skill needed to solve Chen's problem.
